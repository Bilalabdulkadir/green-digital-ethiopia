## 🌍 Overview

**Green Digital Ethiopia** is a sustainability and policy modeling platform designed to evaluate the environmental and policy implications of Ethiopia’s digital infrastructure development — including data centers, cloud operations, and industrial digitalization.

It combines deterministic sustainability modeling with AI‑assisted recommendations to support **research**, **scenario analysis**, and **policy advisory** for green digital transformation.

---

## 🔬 Key Features

- **[Scenario Modeling](ca://s?q=Explain_scenario_modeling_in_Green_Digital_Ethiopia)** — Simulate sustainability outcomes under different infrastructure and policy conditions.  
- **[Regional Policy Analysis](ca://s?q=Describe_regional_policy_analysis_for_Ethiopia)** — Assess how national and regional policies affect digital sustainability.  
- **[AI‑Powered Advisory](ca://s?q=Explain_AI_powered_advisory_using_Gemini)** — Generate recommendations via Gemini for optimized infrastructure planning.  
- **[Deterministic Fallback Guidance](ca://s?q=Explain_deterministic_fallback_guidance)** — Provide evidence‑based policy insights when AI keys are unavailable.  
- **[Interactive Policy Briefs](ca://s?q=Describe_interactive_policy_briefs_feature)** — Create concise, research‑ready summaries for decision‑makers.  
- **[Research Citations](ca://s?q=Add_research_citation_section)** — Integrate academic references and sustainability metrics for publication support.
